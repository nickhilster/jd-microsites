What this role actually is

Knowledge from documents, requirements, architecture artifacts and metadata, modelled and indexed so agents retrieve the right information at the right time — cited, access-controlled, accurate, owned, fresh and traceable.

01

Ground every answer.

Retrieval first, generation second. Answers cite retrieved sources; grounding rules are enforced in the system, not hoped for in the prompt.

02

Govern the knowledge.

Ownership, freshness, completeness and lineage are properties of the hub, maintained by pipelines and review — stale context is a defect, not a surprise.

03

Access follows authorization.

Agents retrieve only what the asking user is authorized to see — role-based access enforced at retrieval time, by design.
