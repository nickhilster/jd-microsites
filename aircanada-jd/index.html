Product Manager - Operations Centre · Toronto

Forward deployed: built with the people who run the operation.

Applied AI is versatile — the same capability can serve a flight dispatcher, a clinic intake worker, or a field technician. But versatility only becomes value when a product is built with the people who operate the end product, inside their workflow, and judged by their operation's metrics. That is my product practice, and it is what an operations centre runs on.

See the evidence The method

The method

Forward deployment, as a loop

01

Embed

Sit with the operators — System Operations Control, Flight Dispatch, Weight and Balance — where the work actually happens, not where the org chart says it does.

02

Observe

Requirements come from live operations: what is late, what is blocked, what changed. User feedback and operational metrics set the priorities.

03

Build

Strategy, roadmap and business case, then delivery with the operators in the loop — prototypes they can react to, not specifications they must imagine.

04

Prove

User acceptance testing, coordinated release, and a metrics review against the numbers that justified the work. Adoption is earned at the desk, not announced.
