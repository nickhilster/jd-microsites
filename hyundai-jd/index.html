The method

How an ecosystem gets built

01

Connect

Unify the data and platforms first. A retrieval layer across 73 documents from 8 sources; an enterprise platform integrating AI search into existing field tools. Ecosystems are integration work before they are channel work.

02

Journey

Design the full funnel as one lifecycle — prospecting, consideration, welcome, ownership, loyalty — so each stage hands the customer, and their context, to the next.

03

Partner

National strategy succeeds or fails at the local counter. Work the seam between head office and the network: shared roadmaps, governance, and tools the network actually adopts.

04

Measure

A measurement framework that ties digital activity to business outcomes — dashboards an operation runs its week on, not reports it files.
