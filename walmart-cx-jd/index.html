What this role actually is

Ownership of the eCommerce NPS and Perfect Order performance framework: the KPIs, the reporting, the root-cause analysis and the improvement plans — plus the regular performance reviews that turn customer pain points and operational gaps across the end-to-end order journey into owned, dated, measurable actions across Operations, Product, Technology, eComm and Supply Chain, and the longer-term strategy and roadmap that compounds the gains. It rewards an operator who treats a score as a system output — and fixes the system.

01

The score is a system output. Fix the system.

NPS moves when the workflows underneath it move. Root-cause work traces the pain point to the process, the handoff, the tool — then the improvement plan fixes that, and the next reporting cycle verifies it.

02

Every finding leaves the room with an owner and a date.

A performance review that ends in observations changes nothing. Ownership, corrective action, progress tracking, a measurable improvement to check next cycle — accountability is the framework’s moving part.

03

Separate what is observed from what is inferred.

Honest frameworks label their evidence: observed, inferred, unknown, human decision. Root cause built on inference presented as fact sends good teams fixing the wrong thing.

First 90 days

DAYS 1–30

Baseline the framework.

Audit the current NPS and Perfect Order framework as it stands: KPI definitions, data sources, reporting cadence, where the end-to-end order journey’s failure modes are already visible. Publish the baseline and the review rhythm.

DAYS 31–60

Run the accountability cycle.

Performance reviews with Operations, Product, Technology, eComm and Supply Chain on the baseline: root-cause the top pain points, assign each opportunity an owner, a corrective action and a date, and track progress in the open.

DAYS 61–90

Verify, then roadmap.

Close the first improvement cycle against its metrics — what moved, what didn’t, what the evidence says next — and set the longer-term NPS and Perfect Order strategy and roadmap from verified results, not opinion.
