The moments

Designed for overwhelming transitions.

Your families are deciding during one of the most meaningful and overwhelming transitions of their lives. So are my users — different transitions, same design bar.

01

Intuitive under stress

A tribunal deadline, a first computer, a scam text at midnight: when the user is frightened, clarity is not a style choice, it is the product. That is the register I design in.

02

Simple when decisions are complex

Registries, benefits forms, legal paperwork — the pattern is the same: sequence the complexity so the human only ever faces one decision at a time.

03

Delightful when the moment calls

Polish is respect. The transitions, the micro-interactions, the empty state that reassures instead of scolding — the details everyone responds to and few can articulate.
