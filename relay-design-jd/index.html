Process

How a product gets from problem to shipped.

ResearchPrimary sources and real users first — orders, records, frontline observation — before a single screen.
FrameName the actual problem in the user's terms; most design failures are framing failures.
DesignSystems before screens: components, patterns and language that scale across the product.
TestAgainst the real thing wherever possible — a live portal, real cases, actual users.
Ship and refineRelease, watch, listen, iterate — with the users inside the loop, not after it.
At Best Buy I did frontline research as a daily practice — hundreds of customers a week — and led a team of 18 to a service turnaround from five business days to under 48 hours. Designing for real people starts with having stood in front of them.
