01

Data integrity is a product feature.

Every funnel metric, loyalty balance and search result inherits the quality of the records beneath it. Seeding discipline, deduplication, orphan cleanup, verification on the failure cases — integrity engineered in, not audited after.

02

The funnel earns the visit it gets.

Traffic and SEO bring the arrival; clarity converts it. Segment by situation, extract what matters, return a tailored path — acquisition as a designed journey, measured at every step.

03

Loyalty is a measurement habit.

Sixteen straight months leading NPS rankings teaches one lesson: loyalty is what the dashboards say customers felt, acted on weekly — not a program description. Voice of customer first, business case second, build third.

First 90 days

DAYS 1–30

Audit & baseline.

Take the Acquisition Funnel & Data Integrity strategy as it stands. With Analytics and Loyalty, baseline the funnel end to end — where traffic stalls, where records decay, where the data feeding decisions cannot be trusted — and agree the metrics that will judge the work.

DAYS 31–60

Experiment & prove.

Run the first discovery cycle with the full cross-functional team: prototypes and experiments against the baselined stalls, each with its ROI case written first and its acceptance criteria explicit. Retire what the evidence retires.

DAYS 61–90

Scale & socialize.

Scale what validated, publish the roadmap and backlog in the open, and socialize the business rationale across Digital Operations, Marketing, Loyalty, Development, Studio, UX and Analytics — one strategy the whole team can repeat.
