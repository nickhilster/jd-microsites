How I work

Three principles, learned where the work happens.

None of these come from a framework deck. They come from carrying products with my own resources on the table.

01

Sit with the customer first.

Problems are uncovered on site and on calls, inside the customer's workflow and economics — not inferred from a roadmap tool. Demand gets validated before development resources get committed.

02

Every roadmap item carries its case.

Revenue potential, development cost, margins, risks — stated plainly enough for an executive team to argue with. A priority you cannot defend numerically is an opinion with a date on it.

03

Launch is the midpoint.

Adoption, customer feedback and commercial performance after launch decide the next round of investment. Strategy that ends at the release announcement is only half the job.
