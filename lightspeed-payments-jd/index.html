What this role actually is

Ownership of a line of business, not stewardship of a feature set: a multi-year Payments strategy tied to OKRs and P&L targets; accountability for penetration, GPV growth, onboarding conversion and line revenue; leadership through Group PMs and Senior PMs across Retail (X-Series, R-Series) and Hospitality (K-Series); and the consolidation of fragmented payments capabilities into one coherent platform, built with Engineering, Design and Commercial partners. The work rewards a leader who has run a business line, priced against a market, and stood at the checkout.

01

Own the funnel, not the feature list.

Penetration is a conversion story: onboarding, activation, share of checkout. Each stage gets an owner, a metric and a product answer — and the stages are managed as one system, not separate backlogs.

02

Consolidate before you expand.

Fragmented capabilities tax every merchant and every team. One coherent platform — shared services, clear build / buy / partner lines — is what lets each product line move faster instead of merely wider.

03

Lead through leaders.

Outcomes scale through Group PMs who own their segments outright: clear commercial targets, coaching on judgment, and accountability that is shared because the ownership is real.

First 90 days

DAYS 1–30

Baseline the funnel.

Map the Payments funnel as merchants experience it — onboarding conversion, activation, share of checkout by platform line — with the Group PMs who own each segment. Counter time with Retail and Hospitality merchants before strategy opinions.

DAYS 31–60

Draw the platform map.

Inventory the fragmented payments capabilities across product lines and draft the consolidation path: what becomes shared platform, what stays local, and the build / buy / partner calls, stated with their trade-offs.

DAYS 61–90

Commit the strategy.

Bring the multi-year Payments strategy — tied to OKRs and P&L targets, with quarterly commitments balanced against platform bets — to the VP Product and executive table, with the measurement cadence that will judge it.
