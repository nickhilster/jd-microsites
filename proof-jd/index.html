How I work

Three principles, from the floor up.

None of these were learned in a design studio. They were learned running services where the exception is the product.

01

Map the ripple before the screen.

Every field action lands in three places at once: the person in the field, the internal team resolving the exception, and the customer watching it happen. Design all three, or the workflow designs itself — badly.

02

Context is a habit, not a phase.

Shadow the operation. Listen to the calls. Sit in the queue. Understanding built daily, while the problem is still open, beats a research phase scheduled after the decisions have quietly been made.

03

Automate with restraint.

The design judgment that matters most in AI-assisted products: where automation creates leverage, and where human judgment, transparency, and a named accountable person matter more. Build both halves deliberately.
