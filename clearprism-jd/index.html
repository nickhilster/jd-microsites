The deployment loop

How I forward-deploy.

BuildThe AI application or workflow, scoped to the scenario it has to survive — not a demo of what the model can do.
IntegrateAPIs connecting the application to the business systems it serves: data, tools and platforms, secured and documented.
DeployInto the real environment, with the people who will use it — launch is the start of the work, not the end.
Test in the wildReal-world scenarios from actual users, evaluated honestly: my client chatbot reached 16 of 16 only after live testing surfaced a fix.
RefineTroubleshoot, tune, re-test. The loop repeats until the solution performs where it is used.
Forward deployment is a discipline of presence: the engineer is in the room, the system is in the field, and the feedback is real.
