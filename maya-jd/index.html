How I work

Three principles of transformation that survives contact.

Learned running AI programs where the metrics were public inside the building and the users did not report to me.

01

No build without a case.

Every initiative starts as a written business case with success metrics agreed by the business unit head before work starts. If the value cannot be stated, the pilot is a hobby.

02

Governance is architecture, not paperwork.

Usage policy and data classification hold when they are built into the systems — approval gates, access control, grounding rules — not when they live in a PDF nobody opens.

03

Adoption is a teaching discipline.

Champions in each unit, training that respects the learner, and results reported in the unit's own numbers. People adopt what demonstrably makes their work better — measured, not estimated.
