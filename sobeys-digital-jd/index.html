Empire runs more than 1,600 stores under banners — Sobeys, Safeway, IGA, Foodland, FreshCo, Thrifty Foods, Lawtons — that customers experience as distinct and local, on platforms that must behave as one: shared design, content, search and release standards, with each banner free to be itself. What this role actually is

Ownership of the technology strategy, delivery, operations and evolution of every customer-facing digital experience platform across Empire’s banners — web, mobile, content, search, design systems and front-end ecosystems — as the primary technology counterpart to Digital Experience, Digital Product, Customer Engagement, Commerce and the banner teams. The work is a balance the posting names directly: innovation and delivery velocity against operational excellence and platform sustainability, inside a product-led operating model. Shared discipline without flattened identity is the job.

01

Shared standards, local character.

One design language, one content architecture, one release and verification discipline — held centrally so every surface stays coherent, and applied loosely enough that each product, and each banner, keeps its own voice and priorities.

02

Reliability is an experience feature.

Availability, performance and verification are not back-office concerns; they are what the customer feels. Production smoke tests against live endpoints, versioned releases, and performance budgets worked as design discipline — in production, every day.

03

Governance that maintains itself.

Standards decay when nothing tends them. Drift detection, human-review routing and an audit trail turn governance from a slide deck into a system property — consistency that survives staff changes and release pressure.

First 90 days

DAYS 1–30

Inventory & baseline.

Map every customer-facing surface across the banners — web, mobile, content, search, design-system components — and baseline what exists: consistency, performance, accessibility and reliability, banner by banner, with the banner and product teams at the table.

DAYS 31–60

Agree the shared layer.

Establish the shared layer first — design-system and component standards, content and search patterns, release and observability practice — agreed with the teams who will live in it, then prove it on one high-traffic journey end to end and measure the before and after.

DAYS 61–90

Scale inside guardrails.

Extend the proven pattern banner by banner, each team owning its local priorities inside shared guardrails, with governance that maintains itself — drift detection and audit, on the DyKnow pattern — so the discipline outlasts the rollout.
