What this role actually is

The accountable lead for McCain’s commercial digital agenda: strategy and roadmaps across Sales, Marketing and Innovation; a portfolio of global and regional initiatives with funding, resourcing and outcomes aligned; technology investment managed to ROI; and digital capabilities — increasingly AI capabilities — adopted by the internal teams and the customers and partners outside the building. The platform choices matter. The adoption and the measured value decide.

01

Connect strategy to execution.

One portfolio view: every commercial initiative with its roadmap, funding, delivery commitments and KPIs. Priorities set openly, revisited as delivery evidence arrives.

02

Adoption is the deliverable.

A capability nobody uses is a cost, not a transformation. Adoption — internal teams, retail customers, partners — is planned, resourced and measured from the first roadmap draft.

03

Measure value, then fund it.

Investment plans, forecasts and ROI tracked as an operating rhythm. What proves value earns scale; what doesn’t gets repositioned early, while the funding can still move.
