For Autodesk Assistant

What I would do with this seat.

Model

One content model for the Assistant

Guidance, knowledge, conversational and learning content as structured, typed, retrievable assets — with the taxonomy and metadata that make an agent's answers traceable to a source someone owns.

Govern

Ownership people can see

Contribution models and decision rights that survive a matrixed org: who authors, who reviews, who approves, who retires — written down, versioned, and enforced by the tooling rather than by meetings.

Evaluate

Quality you can measure

Evaluation frameworks for authored and AI-generated content alike — sourced checks, sampled human review, correction loops — so trust in the Assistant is a metric, not a mood.
