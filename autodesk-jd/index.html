Buildings are data. I design how people read them.

My career began in 3D — team lead at Famous Studios, production coordination at Legend 3D — and was formalized in spatial design at the University of Toronto's XR program. A digital twin is a spatial model carrying live data. That is the intersection I have been walking toward for fifteen years.

For Tandem

What I would do with this seat.

Model

Start from the operator's mental model

Interviews and workshops with the people who run buildings — how they already think about their spaces — before a single screen is drawn. Spatial products fail when the model's logic is the database's, not the user's.

Visualize

Hierarchy for live building data

BIM geometry plus IoT, BMS and CMMS streams is a firehose. The design work is deciding what surfaces, what waits, and what only appears when it matters — depth and emphasis, the 3D instincts, applied to data.

Validate

Prototype with operators, early

Interactive prototypes in front of real users, concept and usability testing every cycle — the practice I run on every product I ship now.
