What this role actually is

An eight-month mandate on a running system: own the personalization strategy and roadmap for Kobo’s eCommerce experience — user segmentation, dynamic content, tailored promotions, the discovery journey — while evolving the recommendation engine with the Data Team and proving each change with experiments. It rewards an operator who can inherit a live engine, baseline it honestly, and move its metrics before handing it back stronger. That is the shape of my practice.

01

Relevance is a ranking problem first.

Before surfaces and shelves, the engine decides what deserves to be seen. Embeddings, similarity thresholds, corpus freshness, failure queries — the ranking discipline underneath the experience, tuned and verified in production.

02

Design for a defined reader, not an average one.

Segments are people with a situation: the tenant mid-crisis, the senior opening an app for the first time, the reader between books. Products built for a defined audience convert and retain; products built for everyone persuade no one.

03

Every recommendation is a hypothesis with a measurement attached.

Hypothesize, practice, validate. Each change ships with the metric that will judge it — engagement, conversion, the next book actually opened — and the verdict writes the next iteration.

The eight months, in three movements

WEEKS 1–4

Inherit & baseline.

Take over the personalization backlog and roadmap as they stand. With the Data Team, baseline the discovery funnel and recommendation performance — where readers stall between browsing and reading — and agree the metrics that will judge the contract.

MONTHS 2–5

Hypothesize, practice, validate.

Run the experiment queue: segmentation refinements, dynamic content, tailored promotions, recommendation tuning — each shipped with its metric attached, iterated on what the data returns, communicated across Data, R&D, UX and Marketing.

MONTHS 6–8

Optimize & hand over.

Scale what validated, retire what didn’t, and document the roadmap, baselines and learnings so the returning incumbent inherits a stronger, legible system — and the next eight months start from evidence, not archaeology.
