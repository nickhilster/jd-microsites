First 90 days

DAYS 1–30

Map & prioritise.

With IT and the functional leaders, map the finance, rights management, production budgeting, payroll and planning systems as they connect today; inventory manual processes and spreadsheet workarounds; rank AI and automation opportunities by productivity, accuracy and decision value, with the COO.

DAYS 31–60

Build the first owned capability.

Stand up the first Cineflix-owned LLM workflow on the highest-ranked use case, with responsible-AI practice agreed with IT, Legal and Privacy; baseline its metrics before launch so benefit is provable, and begin the core-systems modernisation roadmap.

DAYS 61–90

Adopt & measure.

Roll out with communications, training and skills support through HR and business leaders; measure implementation progress, employee adoption and realised benefit; report the first results and the next tranche of the roadmap to the COO.
