What this role actually is

Production Generative AI for research: LLM applications and research agents combining proprietary knowledge with model reasoning — retrieval optimized, answers grounded and cited, quality measured continuously.

01

Optimize retrieval first.

Generation quality is capped by retrieval quality. Chunking, k-values, thresholds and filters are tuned against real questions before a prompt is touched.

02

Ground every answer.

Answers cite retrieved sources. Grounding rules live in the system and are hardened against traced failures — not hoped for in the prompt.

03

Evaluate like it ships.

Evaluation sets drawn from real workflows, smoke tests against the live system, and monitoring for retrieval failures and stale context — from the first release, not after scale.
