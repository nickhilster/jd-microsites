What this role actually is

An enterprise mandate: see one customer whole across six banners and every channel — store, digital, eCommerce, app, marketing — and turn that view into strategy for acquisition, engagement, retention, and lifetime value. An AI roadmap with governance. A loyalty and MarTech platform estate to own as a product. A Centre of Excellence that makes measurement a business habit. It rewards an operator whose measurement was earned in operations and whose AI is already running in production.

01

Measure what the customer actually did.

Dashboards follow the counter, not the other way around. Root-cause the friction customers report, redesign the workflow behind it, and let the numbers — NPS, turnaround, repeat behaviour — render the verdict.

02

Evidence before models.

Settle the KPI definitions and the data behind them first — one governed version of the truth across banners — then build. A model on contested definitions produces confident disagreement, not intelligence.

03

Govern the AI; keep the human the authority.

Every AI system ships with approval points, receipts, and a named human who can stop it. Governance is not a policy document — it is operating practice, running in production.

First 90 days

DAYS 1–30

Baseline the measurement.

Audit the customer KPIs, their definitions, and the data sources behind them across banners. Settle one governed version of the truth before building anything on top of it — and publish the baseline the whole business can read.

DAYS 31–60

One bounded pilot, measured.

Take one high-value loyalty use case and run it as a test-and-learn pilot with the outcome defined up front — measured, not admired — so the Enterprise Loyalty AI Roadmap earns its second step from evidence.

DAYS 61–90

Set the operating rhythm.

Platform ownership rhythm for the loyalty and MarTech estate — roadmap, vendor accountability, service levels — and a Centre of Excellence habit of publishing customer evidence the whole business reads, on a cadence.
