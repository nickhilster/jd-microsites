The challenge · A problem a small business owner might face

A small operator, alone, against an institutional process.

Your challenge asks for a problem a small business owner might face, and how I would solve it. This is the one I already ran toward. Ontario's Landlord and Tenant Board puts small landlords — self-made owners with a unit or two, no staff, no lawyer — and the tenants across from them through a process of dozens of forms, strict deadlines and a filing portal that defeats most first-time users. The professionals bill by the hour. Everyone else faces it alone. I built the guide: LTB Buddy, a conversational AI that is live today.

The problem, framed

Complexity is a tax. It falls hardest on the smallest people in the room.

The settingA tribunal process designed for professionals: form numbers, filing deadlines, a portal that assumes you have done this before.
The peopleSmall landlords running a business of one or two units. Tenants one missed step from losing their home. Legal clinics with a handful of staff serving both.
The gapHelp exists — at professional hourly rates. For everyone else, the instructions are written for someone else.
How I met itFrom inside it, as a participant — the way your owners meet their problems. The framing that survived: this is a design problem, not a knowledge problem.
Nobody assigned me this problem. There was no brief, no client, no funding line. It was broken and unclear, so I stepped in.
What I built

LTB Buddy — live, tested, and used by clinics.

Live · Public beta

The product

A conversational AI that guides people through the tribunal process: which form, which deadline, which step next — plus a browser assistant that walks the actual filing portal step by step. About 50 signups in beta; legal clinics in the pilot cohort use it with real clients.

ltbbuddy.ca →

The research

Earned, not assumed

To build honestly I read and coded 46 adjudicator orders and mapped 26 recurring landlord tactics across 37 more. Primary sources — the system as it actually behaves, not as its website describes it.

The proof

Tested against the real thing

The portal assistant is tested against the live government portal it supports; its suites run green end to end — 55 of 55 in the latest recorded run. Clinics using it find the breaks; the fixes ship from their feedback.
