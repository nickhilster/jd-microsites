What this role actually is

A portfolio directorship, not a product ownership: define a multi-year strategy across Lightspeed’s Instore Selling portfolio for Retail and Hospitality, connect it across product areas, make the investment trade-offs explicit, develop the product leaders who execute it, and hold the whole portfolio accountable for merchant outcomes — satisfaction, adoption, revenue, market share. The work rewards a leader who has run a portfolio, served retailers from the floor up, and shipped AI into real selling moments.

01

Start at the counter.

Every instore strategy claim gets tested where it lands: the associate’s next action, the merchant’s next decision. Store time is not research theatre — it is where the roadmap earns or loses its premises.

02

Run the portfolio, not the loudest product.

Strategy across product areas means explicit trade-offs: where investment compounds, where it merely accumulates. Priorities stated across planning horizons, defended with merchant and business outcomes.

03

Develop leaders who own outcomes.

Product organizations scale through people: coaching, judgment and outcome ownership in the leaders below the strategy. The operating model is the multiplier; the roadmap is only its current expression.

First 90 days

DAYS 1–30

Learn it where it happens.

Store visits and counter time with Retail and Hospitality merchants before roadmap opinions. Map the Instore Selling portfolio as merchants and product leaders experience it; baseline the outcome metrics — satisfaction, adoption, revenue — with the teams who own them.

DAYS 31–60

Connect the strategy.

Draft the multi-year portfolio strategy with its trade-offs stated plainly: where services, AI-driven insights and the instore experience differentiate, and what each product area stops doing. Align it with Engineering, Design, Sales and Marketing leadership.

DAYS 61–90

Commit the operating model.

Convert strategy into objectives, investment priorities and discovery/delivery practices the product leaders own — with the measurement cadence that will judge the portfolio, quarter by quarter, in merchant outcomes.
