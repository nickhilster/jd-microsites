Method

How a case gets decided.

GatherAll of it — transactions, interactions, records — before any view forms.
ValidateAgainst primary sources; a claim is a lead, not a fact.
CompareInconsistencies and gaps are where the case usually turns.
DecidePolicy applied consistently; root cause named, not just the symptom.
ExplainThe client hears it plainly, with empathy and the reasoning shown.
