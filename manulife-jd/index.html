What this role actually is

The single accountable view of GTO’s AI and automation program: strategy translated into prioritized, funded initiatives; delivery, adoption and success criteria watched against approved budgets; and clear executive recommendations on what to accelerate, reposition or stop. Manulife has the platform and the partnerships. This seat supplies the portfolio discipline that turns them into measured enterprise value.

01

One view, or it isn’t governed.

Initiatives, dependencies, funding and outcomes on one page, in one rhythm. Leadership attention goes where the integrated view says it is needed — not where the loudest update points.

02

Prioritization is a funding decision.

Strategic alignment, value, readiness, scalability, risk — scored openly, sequenced honestly, and revisited as delivery evidence arrives. Resources stay on the highest-value opportunities.

03

Value is traced, not asserted.

Every initiative carries its business case, success criteria and budget into delivery — and its realized outcome is checked against them. Provenance is the operating system, not the audit afterthought.

A portfolio is not a list of initiatives. It is a set of promises — each with funding, a delivery commitment, and a value that either arrives or gets explained.

First 90 days

DAYS 1–30

Build the integrated view.

Inventory every GTO AI and automation initiative with its business case, funding, commitments, success criteria and dependencies. Baseline value realized against value promised.

DAYS 31–60

Run the first portfolio review.

Stand up governance rhythms with Product Owners, Segment Leads, Enterprise AI, Finance and Risk. First prioritization pass: accelerate, reposition, deprioritize — with funding recommendations attached.

DAYS 61–90

Make value visible.

Executive reporting cadence live: roadmap progress, funding utilization, delivery performance, value realization, risks and decisions required. First reusable-capability candidates identified for scale across segments.
